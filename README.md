Pennydance is a spending tracker app for Android, made by [CortexZip LLC](https://cortexzip.com/). It is for people who want to know where their money went without linking a bank or being shown ads. You log each purchase yourself in a few seconds, by typing ("oat latte 6.25") or speaking it. Because entries are manual, cash spending counts the same as card spending. The ledger stays on your phone.

- **Platform:** Android, on Google Play now. An iPhone version comes next.
- **Price:** free forever, with optional Plus ($5.99 once) and Premium ($3.99/month or $39.99/year).
- **Ads:** none on any plan, and no ad SDKs.
- **Account:** none needed. No sign-up, no bank login.

## What it does

- **Quick add.** Type or speak a purchase and it's saved in seconds. Cash counts the same as card.
- **Remember what you bought.** Attach a photo, search past purchases ("sunglasses"), and browse them as a list, a grid or a swipeable stack. With Plus, mark who an item was for (a child, a partner, anyone) and tap a name to see only theirs.
- **Subscriptions.** Log one once ("streaming 15.99/mo") and it repeats every cycle. See what's due this month and the yearly total. Plus adds a reminder the day before any renewal. Pennydance does not cancel subscriptions for you.
- **Reports.** Spending by category, day and month, and a calendar. An optional "Spending Power" monthly limit shows what's left.
- **Tax, cashback and promos.** Record sales tax, VAT or GST, cashback and card promos on each entry.
- **Your data stays yours.** Export everything to CSV at any time, and lock the app with your face, fingerprint or screen lock.
- **AI, only when you tap it (Premium).** Turn a messy note into a clean entry (item, quantity, price, store, category), or scan a receipt into line items with a total check.

## Privacy

- The ledger is stored on your phone. There is no account, and our servers do not keep your ledger.
- Nothing is sent anywhere unless you tap an AI button. That request passes through our relay, which keeps no copy (only usage counters), to the AI model, and the entry comes back to your phone. Our AI provider has committed not to train on it.
- Full details: [pennydance.com/privacy](https://pennydance.com/privacy/)

## What it is not

- Not a bank-sync or open-banking app. It never imports transactions automatically.
- Not an envelope budgeting app.
- Not a bank, card or cash management account. It holds no money.

## FAQ

**Does Pennydance connect to my bank account?**
No. You log purchases yourself by typing or speaking them. That is also why cash spending shows up, which bank-sync apps miss.

**Is Pennydance really free?**
Yes. The Free plan is $0 forever with 100 new entries a month, voice quick-add, the subscription list, full reports and CSV export. There are no ads on any plan.

**Where is my spending data stored?**
On your phone. You can lock the app with App Lock and export everything to CSV at any time.

**Is it a budgeting app?**
It is mainly a spending tracker: it records where your money went. If you want a limit, turn on Spending Power, set a monthly amount, and Home shows what is left.

**Is it a good Mint alternative?**
For people who want to log spending by hand without linking a bank, yes. If you need automatic bank sync, pick a bank-sync app instead. See the [Mint alternative](https://pennydance.com/mint-alternative/) page for an honest comparison.

**I got a new phone. Do I keep my plan?**
Yes. Plus and an active Premium subscription come back when you open the app signed in to the same Google Play account. The plans page also has a Restore purchases button.

**Is there an iPhone version?**
Not yet. Pennydance is on Google Play now, and the App Store version comes next.
